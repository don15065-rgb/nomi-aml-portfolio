Practice

I am an Advocate of the High Court, enrolled with the Sindh Bar Council, practising as a civil and criminal lawyer in Karachi from an office in Clifton.

My work runs from the trial courts to the appellate forums: pleadings, interim relief, evidence and appeal. On the civil side that means property and inheritance disputes, suits for declaration, injunction and recovery, and contract matters. On the banking side it means recovery suits before the Banking Courts, execution proceedings and customer claims. On the criminal side it means bail, complaints and trial defence.

I appear before the district courts of Karachi, including South, East, Malir and Korangi, the Banking Courts, the Consumer Protection Courts and the High Court of Sindh.

Before returning to practice in Pakistan, I read for an LLM in International Banking Law and Finance at the University of Edinburgh, graduating with Distinction, after an LLB (Hons) from the University of London. I also hold the CAMS certification in anti-money laundering.

I take the unfashionable view that a lawyer’s first duty to a client is clarity: about the law, about the realistic outcomes, and about the cost of getting there.

What clients can expect

Direct communication, careful review of documents, honest assessment of risks, and legal work prepared with seriousness.
