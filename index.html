About

I am an Advocate of the High Court, enrolled with the Sindh Bar Council, practising as a civil and criminal lawyer in Karachi from an office in Clifton. My work runs from the trial courts to the High Court of Sindh: pleadings, interim relief, evidence and appeal.

Before returning to practice in Pakistan, I read for an LLM in International Banking Law and Finance at the University of Edinburgh, graduating with Distinction, after an LLB from the University of London. That background shapes how I litigate: financial disputes are rarely just about the money claimed, they are about the documents and the regulatory architecture sitting behind the transaction.

I take the unfashionable view that a lawyer’s first duty to a client is clarity: about the law, about the realistic outcomes, and about the cost of getting there.
